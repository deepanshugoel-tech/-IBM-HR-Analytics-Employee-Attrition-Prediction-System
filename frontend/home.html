About the company

We build tools that turn HR data into decisions.

IBM HR started with a simple problem: companies lose experienced people and only find out when the resignation arrives. By then the reasons, such as overtime, a stalled promotion or a long commute, have been building for months.

Our platform studies past employee records, predicts who is at risk, and shows HR which factors matter most for each person. Managers get a short, ranked list instead of a spreadsheet, and employees get a place to view their own profile.
